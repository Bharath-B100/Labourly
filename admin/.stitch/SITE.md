# Site Vision & Roadmap

## 1. Vision
NammaCrew Operations Dashboard. A robust, fast, and modern internal tool for managing customers, workers, verifications, requirements, and bookings for the NammaCrew platform.

## 2. Target Audience
Internal operations team, administrators, and support staff.

## 3. Core Principles
- Data density over whitespace (without being cluttered)
- Fast navigation
- Clear states and actions
