# Labourly privacy and retention — owner review draft

Not published. Not a completed privacy policy or legal approval.

Keep the initial process simple: requests go through Profile → Support requests & account deletion. Users can see the request status and support response. Email fallback: labourly.work@gmail.com.

The public policy must truthfully describe account identifiers and authentication, entered contact numbers, profiles and service coverage, device location permission and manual selection, requirements/quotations/bookings, messages, support requests, technical logs, and Google/hosting/push providers actually used. Do not claim anonymous processing or end-to-end message encryption.

Explain which location details are public, which are shown only to authorized booking participants, and how permission denial is handled, based on verified endpoint behavior. The current app wording is not a substitute for this review.

Before deletion fulfillment can be enabled, Labourly must decide and document:
- Who operates the support inbox and verifies ownership of external deletion requests.
- How active bookings, unpaid disputes and safety complaints are handled.
- Which booking/audit records must be retained, for what specific reason and period.
- What profile/contact/authentication/message data is deleted or anonymized, and how counterparties' rights are preserved.
- When backup copies expire and how restoration reapplies deletion decisions.
- How the requester is told what was removed, what was retained and why.

No retention period has been invented or approved in this draft. Do not publish placeholders or claim all data is instantly deleted.

Provide a public HTTPS deletion-request page that works without installing the app, plus an in-app request option. The current authenticated intake is only one part of this requirement. Complete Google Play Data safety declarations against actual data flows and the final published policy.
